# Giftus Design System

## World
Intimate Indian celebration pages. The registry feels like a personal invitation wall — family photos lead, gifts live inside the story. Not a SaaS wishlist. Not Gifaa.

## Palette
| Token | Hex | Role |
|-------|-----|------|
| paper | `#FBF7F2` | Page ground |
| ink | `#1C1410` | Primary text |
| rose | `#B84A5E` | Brand / CTA / love accent |
| petal | `#F8E8EC` | Soft wash |
| sage | `#4A5D4E` | Secondary calm |
| gold | `#A8894A` | Celebration highlight |
| mist | `#EFE8E1` | Borders / subtle fills |

Avoid: purple gradients, terracotta `#D97757`, Inter/Roboto, generic card grids in the hero.

## Type
- Display: **Fraunces** — soft optical serif for brand & registry titles
- Body / UI: **Figtree** — clear, friendly, mobile-readable

## Motion
- Landing: one orchestrated hero reveal
- Registry: sparse floating hearts; purchase highlights pulse once
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Layout principles
- Brand first in the hero
- Full-bleed emotional photography on registry covers
- Cards only for interactive gift / fund units
- Mobile-first; iOS UPI flows get QR + app buttons + copy
