# Giftus

India's premium celebration gift registry — personalized pages for baby showers, weddings, and life's milestones.

## Audience
- Registry creators (parents-to-be, couples) who want a beautiful, personal page — not a generic wishlist
- Guests (family, friends, often on iPhone) who need zero-friction gifting

## Purpose
Share exactly what you want, with your story and photos, so loved ones gift with joy — via store links or direct UPI — without duplicates or confusion.

## Voice
Warm, intimate, clear. Celebration language, never corporate SaaS. Short sentences. Hindi-English mix welcome in user content; product UI in simple English.

## Modes
- Marketing / landing: Persuade
- Owner dashboard: Operate
- Public registry page: Experience (the family's page is the product)
